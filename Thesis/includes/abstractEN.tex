The objective of this bachelor's thesis was to design and implement a small robotic vehicle capable of autonomous driving and obstacle detection using modern computer vision techniques. The vehicle was equipped with an ESP32-CAM camera module and an ultrasonic sensor that continuously monitored the area ahead, providing data for a Python-based control server. A key element of the solution involved image processing via the OpenCV library, where segmentation and contour detection within a defined region of interest ensured the vehicle remained centered along the track. To enhance safety and identify complex obstacles, the YOLOv8 convolutional neural network was integrated into the system, which, combined with distance measurements, allowed the vehicle to respond smoothly to objects. The entire hardware subsystem was managed by the Arduino platform, with wireless communication conducted via HTTP and UDP protocols.
